## The entry

> Imagine a road of light joining every post office and library on earth, so a letter you write here reaches a stranger across the sea before the ink is dry, and their reply returns just as fast.

One sentence, 193 characters. It explains the modern internet to someone
arriving from 1800 using only things they already know — a road, a post
office, a library, a letter, ink — and one picture they can hold in their
head: a road of light carrying letters across the sea before the ink dries.
